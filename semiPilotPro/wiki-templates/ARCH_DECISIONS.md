# Architecture Decisions

## Summary
Architecture Decision Records — why we chose X over Y.

## Tags

## Context
Every non-trivial architectural choice gets an ADR here. Scribe appends on every approved implementation plan that authorized one. Supersede entries rather than deleting — history matters for future agents.
